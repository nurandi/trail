Finding the Right Route

Cari Rute yang Cocok

This archive keeps growing. Use the filters below to quickly find routes that match your plan: easy runs, long efforts, or race simulations.
Always monitor natural conditions and obey the latest signs/boards on the trail.

Koleksi rute di sini terus bertambah. Gunakan filter untuk menemukan rute yang sesuai, baik untuk lari santai, long run, atau simulasi lomba.
Selalu pantau kondisi alam dan patuhi petunjuk yang ada di rute (misal papan petunjuk, dll).

Search

Pencarian

Type a route name, location, or keyword (for example, "Sentul") to instantly narrow the list.

Ketik nama rute, lokasi, atau kata kunci (misalnya "Sentul") untuk langsung menyaring hasil.

Distance & Elevation

Jarak & Elevasi

The basics: how far you’ll run and how much you’ll climb. Elevation gain is rounded so it’s easy to read at a glance.

Info dasar: seberapa jauh dan seberapa nanjak. Angka elevasi dibulatkan supaya gampang dibaca.

Effort Score (km effort)

Effort Score (km effort)

A quick way to tell how hard a route feels. Calculated as: distance (km) + elevation gain (m ÷ 100).
Example: 10 km + 500m vert ≈ 15 Effort.

Cara cepat buat tahu seberapa berat rutenya. Dihitung dengan rumus: jarak (km) + (total elevasi meter ÷ 100).
Contoh: 10 km + 500m vert ≈ 15 Effort.

Activity Type

Jenis Aktivitas

Know what you’re looking at: Races (official events), Training (personal runs), or Routes (planned routes imported from Strava).

Biar jelas ini rute apa: Race (lomba resmi), Training (lari latihan), atau Route (rute rencana dari Strava).

Recency

Update Terakhir

Want the most up-to-date trail conditions? Filter routes by how recently they were run.

Mau kondisi trail paling update? Filter berdasarkan kapan rute dibuat atau terakhir dilewati.

Is This Trail Still Good?

Rute Masih Layak?

Trails change over time—rain, mud, and vegetation can affect conditions. Color labels help you judge how fresh the data is.

Kondisi trail bisa berubah karena hujan, lumpur, atau vegetasi. Label warna membantu menunjukkan seberapa baru data rute.

< 1 Week < 1 Minggu

Super fresh. Just ran it, conditions should be accurate.

Super fresh. Baru dijalankan, kondisi masih akurat.

< 1 Month < 1 Bulan

Still fresh. Usually not much has changed.

Masih oke. Biasanya belum banyak berubah.

< 1 Year < 1 Tahun

Generally reliable. Route is solid, but check recent weather.

Umumnya aman. Jalur masih jelas, tapi tetap cek cuaca terakhir.

> 1 Year > 1 Tahun

Use caution. Trails may be overgrown or damaged.

Perlu ekstra hati-hati. Jalur bisa tertutup atau rusak.

Tip: Hover over the time label to see exactly when the route was last run. Tips: Arahkan kursor ke label waktu untuk melihat tanggal pastinya.
